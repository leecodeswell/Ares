Our kitchen

A tiny shop with
very particular habits.

Eleanor Hollis and her sister Rose opened the shop with one oven and their grandmother’s recipe box. Ten years on, there are three ovens and the same rules: real butter, nothing from a packet, and nothing leaves the kitchen that we wouldn’t serve at our own table.
